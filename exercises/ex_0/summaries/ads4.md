    *   Experience leading data architecture work and translating business strategy into technical roadmaps.
    *   Strong understanding of data security, privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR), and compliance best practices.
    *   Ability to define and drive data quality frameworks, including automated testing and validation processes.
    *   Experience with real-time or streaming data processing technologies (e.g., Kafka, Kinesis).

    *   Experience with tools and systems such as DBT, Airflow, or Fivetran.
